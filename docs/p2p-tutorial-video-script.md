# Purpose 2 Profit — Learning Journey Tutorial (Video Script)

A shot-by-shot script for a ~2–3 minute screen-capture walkthrough. Record on the
**Journey page** while logged in (so real stats show). Screen-record at 1280×800 or
larger; keep the cursor visible; move slowly and pause on each element.

**Tone:** warm, encouraging, unhurried — like a friend showing you around, not a
manual. Feel free to reword the voiceover in your own voice.

---

### Scene 1 — Warm welcome (0:00–0:12)
**On screen:** The Journey page hero — the title and the Daily Mindset Moment card.
**Say:**
> "Welcome to your Learning Journey. This is your home base — your map from wherever
> you're starting all the way to a business that runs. Let me give you a quick tour."

### Scene 2 — The map (0:12–0:30)
**On screen:** Slowly pan/scroll across the board. Hover a glowing marker, then a
locked one.
**Say:**
> "This board is your trail. Every glowing marker is a course. Finish one and the
> path lights up to the next — so you can always see how far you've come. Markers
> that are locked simply open up as you clear the ones before them."

### Scene 3 — Opening a course (0:30–0:52)
**On screen:** Click a glowing marker → the course opens. Show marking a lesson
complete, then the completion celebration + certificate.
**Say:**
> "Tap any glowing marker to jump in. Inside, you work through the lessons and check
> each one off. Finish them all and the course is complete — you earn points, your
> certificate is ready, and the next step on the trail unlocks."

### Scene 4 — Getting around: the tabs (0:52–1:05)
**On screen:** Back on the Journey page. Hover along the nav tabs left to right —
Journey Map, All Courses, Progress, Bonuses, Milestones, Journal.
**Say:**
> "Up here are your tabs. They move you between your map, a full list of every
> course, your progress, bonus check-ins, your badges, and your journal."

### Scene 5 — All Courses (1:05–1:18)
**On screen:** Click **All Courses**. Scroll the realm-grouped list; expand a realm.
**Say:**
> "‘All Courses' lists everything across all five realms, grouped and showing your
> progress — so you can jump straight to any course without hunting on the map."

### Scene 6 — Your Progress (1:18–1:45)
**On screen:** Click **Progress**. Click each tile in turn — Courses, Points,
Badges, Streak, Merit — showing each pop-up for a beat.
**Say:**
> "The Progress tab is your dashboard. Courses done, points, badges, your day streak,
> and your Merit rank. And every tile opens up — tap Points to see exactly where they
> came from, tap Merit to see the whole rank ladder and how close you are to the next
> one. It's your progress, made visible."

### Scene 7 — Bonuses, Milestones, Journal (1:45–2:10)
**On screen:** Click **Bonuses** (show a pulse check), then **Milestones** (show the
badge wall), then **Journal** (add a quick entry).
**Say:**
> "Between courses, the Bonuses tab has quick Mindset, Purpose and Heart check-ins —
> never required, just there to keep you steady. Milestones is your badge wall, filling
> in as you go. And the Journal is your private space to capture notes and wins — your
> first entry each day even earns points."

### Scene 8 — Streaks, weekly goal & recap (2:10–2:35)
**On screen:** Back on Progress. Point to the streak tile and weekly-goal card, then
click **See my Journey Recap** and let the recap card sit on screen.
**Say:**
> "Show up each day to build a streak — and don't worry, one missed day is forgiven.
> There's a gentle weekly goal too. And any time you want to see the big picture — or
> share it — open your Journey Recap for a snapshot of everything you've accomplished."

### Scene 9 — Close (2:35–2:50)
**On screen:** Return to the map. Cursor rests on a glowing marker.
**Say:**
> "That's the tour. Everything's here whenever you need it — and you can replay the
> guided tour any time from the Help menu. Now tap a glowing marker, and let's begin.
> Your journey starts here."

---

**Production notes**
- If you'd rather not narrate live, record the screen silently and add the lines as
  captions, or read them into a voice memo and lay it over the footage.
- Re-shoot only the scene that changed if the UI updates — scenes are self-contained.
- The same nine beats power the in-page **guided tour** (the "Take the tour" button)
  and the **annotated tutorial page** (P2P Tutorial section), so all three stay
  consistent.

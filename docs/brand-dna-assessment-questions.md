# The Founder Interview™ v1.0

The proprietary discovery engine behind the Brand DNA Blueprint™.
Uncovers a founder's natural motivations, values, communication style,
aesthetic preferences, and decision-making patterns, then translates
those patterns into a complete Brand Identity and Brand Compass. The
interview does not measure personality. It measures alignment.

## Interview Architecture

- **Total questions**: 30
- **Estimated completion time**: 17–25 minutes
- **Response format**: multiple choice, 4–5 curated responses per
  question, no "correct" answers
- **Emotional pacing**: intentionally conversational, not clinical.
  Emotional intensity rises and falls throughout rather than clustering —
  light questions alternate with deeper reflection, which builds trust,
  reduces fatigue, and produces more authentic responses than a straight
  run of psychologically heavy questions would.

## The Four Invisible Layers

Every question contributes to up to four independent scoring systems at
once. The founder never sees these layers directly — only the results.

**Layer One — Brand Expression™.** Direct, literal picks for outward
appearance: Photography, Typography, Color Direction, Mood, Voice,
Messaging Style, Brand Experience. Used when an answer maps almost
1:1 to a real field (e.g. "warm earthy neutrals" *is* a color-family
suggestion — no inference needed).

**Layer Two — Brand Tensions™.** The 8 tensions from `brand-dna-framework.md`
(Warmth↔Authority, Freedom↔Purpose, Tradition↔Innovation,
Community↔Recognition, Structure↔Expression, Calm↔Energy,
Accessibility↔Luxury, Playfulness↔Sophistication). Identifies where the
founder naturally falls across each continuum — used for nearest-match
Profile Library lookups, where nuance and blending matter more than a
literal pick.

**Layer Three — Founder DNA™.** 12 independent motivational dimensions:
Purpose, Legacy, Belonging, Freedom, Recognition, Creativity, Security,
Excellence, Impact, Stewardship, Growth, Service. Powers the Mission
Statement, Core Values, Brand Promise, Brand North Star, Decision
Filters, and Founder Narrative — the text/meaning outputs, not the visual
ones.

**Layer Four — Customer Impression™.** Added in v1.1 to close a gap
identified against Kapferer's Brand Identity Prism: Layers One-Three all
measure the *founder* (what the brand looks like, where the founder sits
on each tension, what motivates them). Nothing measured how the founder
wants the *customer* to experience the brand — Kapferer's Relationship,
Reflection, and Self-Image facets. Four dimensions, each a small closed
set of labels rather than a spectrum or open magnitude — every answer
casts a weighted vote for one label, and the highest-voted label per
dimension becomes the result:
- **Self-Image** — how the customer feels about themselves after
  engaging with the brand: capable, understood, bold, refined, grounded.
- **Reflection** — who the brand makes the customer look like to others:
  insider, accomplished, bold, tasteful, connected.
- **Relationship** — the role the brand plays in the customer's life:
  mentor, companion, authority, indulgence, utility.
- **Differentiation** — what stays true even if a competitor copied
  everything: people, story, point of view, standard, community.

## The 30 Questions

**1. Pick the space that feels most like your brand.**
A. Minimalist art gallery / B. Cozy cabin / C. Lively street market / D. Sleek modern office
*Measures: Expression, Brand Tensions*

**2. Imagine your business closed its doors forever, five years from now. What would hurt the most about seeing it disappear?**
A. The people I helped would lose something meaningful / B. The creativity and ideas would disappear / C. The community we built would no longer exist / D. The standard of quality we set would be gone / E. I'd regret never reaching the impact I knew was possible
*Measures: Founder DNA, Brand Tensions*

**3. If your brand were a color palette...**
A. Deep jewel tones / B. Warm earthy neutrals / C. Bright, punchy colors / D. Black, white, and one bold accent
*Measures: Expression*

**4. You win the lottery tomorrow. You never have to work again. Do you still build this business?**
A. Absolutely, this is my purpose / B. Yes, but I'd slow down / C. Maybe — I love creating more than running a business / D. Probably not
*Measures: Founder DNA*

**5. Pick the photography style that feels most "you."**
A. Bright, candid, energetic / B. Soft, warm natural light / C. Moody and dramatic / D. Clean and minimal
*Measures: Expression*

**6. When your customers describe you to a friend, what do you secretly hope they say?**
A. They changed my life / B. They're unbelievably talented / C. I've never trusted anyone more / D. Everything they touch is beautiful / E. They're just genuinely good people
*Measures: Founder DNA, Brand Tensions*

**7. Which pace best describes your brand?**
A. Calm and steady / B. Fast-paced and exciting / C. Deliberate and precise / D. Playful and spontaneous
*Measures: Brand Tensions*

**8. Which failure would hurt you the most?**
A. Creating something nobody remembers / B. Letting down the people who trusted me / C. Never reaching my full potential / D. Becoming just another business / E. Sacrificing my values for success
*Measures: Founder DNA, Brand Tensions*

**9. What should your brand voice sound like?**
A. Trusted friend / B. Confident expert / C. Witty best friend / D. Luxury concierge
*Measures: Expression*

**10. Which compliment would stay with you forever?**
A. You gave me hope / B. You made me believe in myself / C. You created something unforgettable / D. You built something that will outlive you / E. You never compromised who you were
*Measures: Founder DNA, Brand Tensions*

**11. What's your relationship with tradition?**
A. Respect craftsmanship / B. Break the mold / C. Use what works / D. Follow my heart
*Measures: Brand Tensions, Expression*

**12. Imagine your customers sitting around a campfire after you've gone home. What do you hope they're saying?**
A. I've never felt more understood / B. They're changing the game / C. Everything they create is beautiful / D. I wish every company cared this much / E. I can't wait to see what they do next
*Measures: Founder DNA, Brand Tensions*

**13. What matters most in how your brand looks?**
A. Timeless / B. Cutting edge / C. Warm and approachable / D. Polished and elevated
*Measures: Expression, Brand Tensions*

**14. Which sentence scares you the most?**
A. I lived safely / B. I blended in / C. I never made a difference / D. I disappointed people / E. I never became who I was capable of becoming
*Measures: Founder DNA, Brand Tensions*

**15. Which matters more?**
A. Standing out / B. Belonging / C. Staying true to myself / D. Being respected
*Measures: Brand Tensions, Founder DNA*

**16. Finish this sentence. Success means...**
A. Freedom / B. Helping people / C. Making my family proud / D. Becoming known for excellence / E. Leaving the world better than I found it
*Measures: Founder DNA*

**17. If your brand had a soundtrack...**
A. Acoustic / B. Upbeat pop / C. Cinematic / D. Jazz/classical
*Measures: Expression*

**18. A decision promises rapid growth, but it challenges one of your core values. Which response feels most natural?**
A. My values come first / B. I'd look for another path / C. I'd weigh the long-term impact carefully / D. It depends on what value is being challenged / E. If it serves the greater mission, I'd consider it
*Measures: Founder DNA, Brand Tensions*

**19. What energy should every customer interaction leave behind?**
A. Calm / B. Excited / C. Efficient / D. Personally connected
*Measures: Brand Tensions, Expression*

**20. Pick one word that describes the future of your brand.**
A. Timeless / B. Beloved / C. Iconic / D. Elevated
*Measures: Brand Tensions, Founder DNA*

**21. When someone finishes using what you make, what do you most want them to feel about themselves?**
A. Capable — like they can handle more than they thought / B. Cared for — like someone finally gets them / C. Bold — like they took a risk and it paid off / D. Refined — like they have great taste / E. Grounded — like they're exactly who they're supposed to be
*Measures: Customer Impression (Self-Image)*

**22. If your customer could only keep one feeling from working with you, what would you want it to be?**
A. Confidence / B. Relief / C. Excitement / D. Pride / E. Peace
*Measures: Customer Impression (Self-Image)*

**23. Picture your ideal customer a week after buying from you. What's different about how they see themselves?**
A. They believe they're capable of more / B. They feel understood for the first time / C. They feel like they took a bold step forward / D. They feel like they finally have something that matches their taste / E. They feel more like themselves, not less
*Measures: Customer Impression (Self-Image)*

**24. When someone sees your customer using what you make, what do you want them to think?**
A. "They know something the rest of us don't" / B. "They really have their life together" / C. "They're not afraid to stand out" / D. "They have impeccable taste" / E. "They're part of something bigger than themselves"
*Measures: Customer Impression (Reflection)*

**25. What kind of person do you want to be visibly associated with your brand?**
A. The quiet expert everyone trusts / B. The one who has it together / C. The trailblazer nobody expected / D. The person with taste everyone envies / E. The connector who brings people in
*Measures: Customer Impression (Reflection)*

**26. If your brand were a person in your customer's life, who would it be?**
A. The trusted mentor who's seen it all / B. The best friend who always shows up / C. The expert they hire because they don't do it themselves / D. The indulgence they save up for / E. The tool they reach for without thinking twice
*Measures: Customer Impression (Relationship)*

**27. What do you want your customer to feel when they think of reaching out to you?**
A. "They'll guide me" / B. "They'll get me" / C. "They know more than I do about this" / D. "This is my treat" / E. "This just works, every time"
*Measures: Customer Impression (Relationship)*

**28. If a competitor copied your product or service exactly, what would still be different?**
A. The way we treat people / B. The story behind why we started / C. The taste and point of view we bring / D. The standard we hold ourselves to / E. The community we've built
*Measures: Customer Impression (Differentiation)*

**29. Imagine your closest competitor. What's the one thing you refuse to do the way they do it?**
A. Cut corners to move faster / B. Treat customers like transactions instead of people / C. Follow trends instead of our own point of view / D. Sacrifice our values for growth / E. Build alone instead of building community
*Measures: Customer Impression (Differentiation)*

**30. (Signature Closing Question) Imagine someone is telling the story of your business twenty years from now. What do you hope they remember most?**
A. The lives we changed / B. The courage to do things differently / C. The community we built together / D. The standard of excellence we refused to compromise / E. That we stayed true to who we were from beginning to end
*Measures: Founder DNA (Primary), Brand Tensions (Secondary), Brand Expression (Validation)*

Question 30 (formerly Question 21, moved rather than rewritten when the
interview expanded from 21 to 30 questions) is intentionally untouchable
— no future revision should water it down or move it from the closing
position. When someone finishes the interview, they shouldn't remember a
question about colors. They should remember the one that made them pause
and think about the legacy they hope to leave. That's the bridge between
the assessment and everything the Blueprint builds from it.
